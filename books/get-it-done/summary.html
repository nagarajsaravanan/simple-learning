The Zoho Master Action Plan

"Success is the natural byproduct of a well-engineered daily system."

Here is the complete 1-Page Operating Playbook integrating all 12 chapters from Dr. Ayelet Fishbach's Get It Done directly into your daily routine to crack Zoho:

The 4-Pillar Daily System

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   THE DAILY ZOHO SUCCESS SYSTEM                         │
├──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────────────┤
│ 1. MINDSET (Ch 1-4)  │ 2. PACING (Ch 5-7)   │ 3. FOCUS (Ch 8-10)       │
│ Focus on the PRIZE,  │ 7-Day Sprints.       │ Phone in another room.   │
│ not chores. Target   │ Short middles.       │ Highlight coding above   │
│ 2-4 problems/day.    │ 1-Line Post-Mortems  │ secondary goals.         │

├──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┤

│ Explain logic out loud to a peer • Teach to learn • Collaborative mind │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

The 3-Step Daily Routine

Step 1: Night-Before Setup (Zero Friction - Ch 9)

  • Pick tomorrow's 2 target problems before going to sleep.
  • Open your code editor and have your notebook ready on your desk.

Step 2: The Deep Work Block (90 Minutes - Ch 2 & 4)

  • Phone in another room.
  • Listen to your dedicated focus playlist with tea/coffee.
  • Write the logic with basic loops and conditions (avoid relying on built-in helpers).
  • Test with edge cases (empty strings, single elements, negative numbers, boundary limits).

Step 3: The 1-Line Post-Mortem (Ch 7)

  • Did you hit a bug or miss a boundary condition?
  • Write a 1-sentence note in your learning journal:
    Example: "Always check i < length - 1 before accessing arr[i + 1] to prevent index errors."

Complete 12-Chapter Directory

Part I: Choose Your Goal

  1. Ch 1: Goals Aren't Chores — Focus on the prize (Zoho offer), not the chore. Play to win.
  2. Ch 2: Put a Number on It — Use target ranges (2 to 4 problems) and measure outputs.
  3. Ch 3: Incentives Matter — Beware of fake metrics (the Cobra Effect); reward deep struggle.
  4. Ch 4: Intrinsic Motivation — Make daily coding playful; bundle fun with effort.

Part II: Keep the Momentum

  1. Ch 5: The Glass Half Full & Half Empty — Look back at the start; look forward when the finish line is near.
  2. Ch 6: The Middle Problem — Crush mid-project slumps with 7-day sprints.
  3. Ch 7: Learning from Negative Feedback — Treat errors as GPS reroutes; 1-line post-mortems.

Part III: Juggling Goals (Self-Control & Patience)

  1. Ch 8: Goal Juggling — Highlighting coding as your seasonal priority; multi-finality wins.
  2. Ch 9: Self-Control — Friction engineering; pre-commitment contracts.
  3. Ch 10: Patience — Overcoming present bias; channel nervous energy into writing code.

Part IV: The Social Side of Goals

  1. Ch 11: Pursuing Goals with Others — Ambition contagion; explain code out loud.
  2. Ch 12: Goals in Relationships & Teams — The giving-advice paradox; Zoho's team-first culture.
