Featured: You Don't Know JS Yet: Get Started (2nd Edition)

Master the core foundations and mental models of JavaScript:

  • Chapter 1: What Is JavaScript?
    Origins (Mocha/LiveScript), TC39 committee, backwards compatibility, and why JS is a compiled language.
  • Chapter 2: Surveying JS
    Primitives vs Objects, typeof quirks, var/let/const, equality (== vs ===), and Classes vs Modules.
  • Chapter 3: Digging to the Roots of JS
    The engine of JS: Iteration protocols, Closures (backpack analogy), the this keyword demystified, and Prototype Delegation.
  • Chapter 4: The Bigger Picture
    The 3 Pillars of JavaScript, coding "with the grain", and the 6-book series roadmap.
  • Appendix: Practice Challenges & Solutions
    Value vs Reference copies, plus hands-on solutions to the 3 coding challenges (scheduleMeeting, range, and the slot machine).
