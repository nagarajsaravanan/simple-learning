# Chapter 12: Goals in Relationships & Teams

> *"We achieve our biggest goals not by competing with everyone around us, but by coordinating our efforts with people who share our values."*  
> — **Dr. Ayelet Fishbach**

---

## 1. Shared Goals vs. Individual Goals

In the final chapter of *Get It Done*, Dr. Fishbach examines how goals operate within **partnerships, families, and engineering teams**:

* **Independent Goals:** Two people pursuing separate things side-by-side.
* **Shared Goals:** Two or more people aligning their efforts toward a single shared outcome (e.g., shipping a software product, building a family, creating an open-source project).

When goals are truly shared, success feels twice as rewarding and setbacks are shared rather than endured alone.

---

## 2. The "Giving Advice" Paradox

One of the most fascinating discoveries in motivational science:  
**Giving advice to someone else increases your own motivation more than receiving advice!**

* When you receive advice, your brain feels passive, inferior, and slightly helpless.
* When you **give advice** or explain a concept to a junior peer:
  1. Your brain is forced to structure the knowledge cleanly.
  2. You feel capable and confident.
  3. You feel a strong psychological pressure to follow your own advice!

> **The Zoho Prep Hack:** If you want to master a tough concept (like Prototype Delegation or Recursive Backtracking), teach it to a fellow student or write a simple blog post about it. Teaching is the ultimate masterclass.

---

## 3. The Zoho Engineering Culture Connection

Zoho is famous in the software industry for its unique, bootstrapped culture:
* They don't hire lone-wolf coders who write unreadable code and ignore others.
* They prize **humility, collaborative problem-solving, clean communication, and team-first ownership**.
* In Round 4 (Tech HR) and Round 5 (HR), interviewers evaluate how well you collaborate, listen, receive feedback, and align with a team.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways

1. **Teach to learn:** Explaining concepts to peers boosts your own mastery and motivation.
2. **Align on shared missions:** Partner with people who share your high work ethic.
3. **Engineering is a team sport:** Zoho rewards engineers who communicate with clarity, empathy, and humility.
